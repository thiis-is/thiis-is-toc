# Cookie Policy

{{company.service}} does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We rely on a small amount of first-party browser storage, split into essential and optional.

## Essential (always on)

- **Authentication** — Firebase Authentication stores your session so you stay signed in; the dashboard holds a short-lived token in session storage.
- **Preferences** — your dark-mode/theme choice and, on this site, your recently shortened links.

These are necessary to provide the service and cannot be switched off.

## Optional (with your consent)

- **Diagnostics analytics** — Sentry performance tracing and Session Replay, used to diagnose errors and improve reliability. These run only if you accept.

Third-party fonts (Google Fonts) are loaded on the dashboard to render the interface; they set no tracking cookies.

## Your choice

You can accept or reject optional analytics at any time using the consent control on this page. Changes to analytics consent take effect on your next page load.
