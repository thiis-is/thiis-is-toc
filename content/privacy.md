# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how {{company.name}} ("we"), the data controller, processes your personal data when you use {{company.service}}, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable law.

## 1. Controller & contact

{{company.name}}, {{company.address}} ({{company.jurisdiction}}), registration no. {{company.regNo}}. For any privacy matter or to exercise your rights, contact [{{company.privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{company.privacyEmail}}).

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data** — your email address and display name (via Google or email/password sign-in).
- **Link data** — the URLs you shorten and related metadata (labels, tags, folders, QR codes).
- **Click analytics** — for links you own we record aggregate, non-identifying click data: approximate country/region/city (derived at the edge), referrer, device type, browser, operating system, and language. **We do not store your visitors' IP addresses or raw user-agent strings.**
- **Diagnostics** — error and (with your consent) performance/session diagnostics via our processor Sentry, configured not to attach IP addresses.
- **Billing data** — if you purchase a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe; we store your plan, subscription status, and Stripe identifiers, not your card details.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performance of a contract** — to create your account and provide the service and its core analytics.
- **Legitimate interests** — to secure, debug, and improve the service (e.g. error reporting), balanced against your rights.
- **Consent** — for optional analytics (Session Replay and performance tracing); you can withdraw it at any time.
- **Legal obligation** — to comply with law, including sanctions and tax obligations.

## 4. Processors & international transfers

We share personal data only with service providers ("processors") that process it on our behalf under contract:

- **Google / Firebase** — authentication and the redirect data store.
- **Cloudflare** — edge delivery, redirects, and aggregate click analytics/storage (EU jurisdiction).
- **Google Cloud** — application hosting and the primary database (EU region).
- **Sentry** — error and optional performance/session diagnostics (EU ingestion).
- **Stripe** — payment processing for paid plans.

Where a processor transfers data outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Retention

We keep account and link data for as long as your account is active. Aggregate click logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted. We delete or anonymise personal data when it is no longer needed, subject to legal retention requirements. You can delete your account at any time.

- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for us, the {{company.supervisoryAuthority}}, or your local authority.

To exercise any right, email [{{company.privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{company.privacyEmail}}).
